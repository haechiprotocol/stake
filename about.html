Meaning & Function: Believed to be the ultimate symbol of justice, a protector against evil spirits, a warden against natural disasters (especially fires), and an arbiter distinguishing right from wrong.

HISTORICAL ORIGIN

Joseon Dynasty (1390s)

Stone statues of Haechi were erected in front of Gwanghwamun Gate during the Joseon Dynasty (1390s) to guard the royal palace and uphold absolute legal justice.

MODERN DESIGNATION

Symbol of Seoul (2008)

In 2008, Haechi was officially designated as the official symbol and mascot of Seoul. Today, this legacy of protection inspires our global DeFi protocol.

02 // BIND SPONSOR & NODE PRESALE MECHANICS

Single Permanent On-Chain Tree & Node Presale

Haechi Protocol V2 implements a permanent referral structure stored 100% on-chain before public staking opens.

RULE 3.1

Binding Required Before Transacting

New users connecting their Web3 wallet without an existing sponsor must execute bindSponsor() before purchasing Nodes or Staking. Entering via a referral link automatically fills the sponsor input. Once bound initially, this referral structure remains permanent throughout the staking phase.

RULE 3.2

Zero-Barrier to Refer & Anti-Loop

No Purchase Required to Refer: Users who have not purchased Nodes can still share their referral link and build teams immediately, protected by an automated Anti-Circular Loop safeguard that prevents (A → B → A) referral anomalies.

Node purchases distribute 3% (Level 1) and 2% (Level 2) USDT referral bonuses from base spend, completely independent of the 300% staking cap:

06 // GAS FEE OPTIMIZATION & MULTI-LAYER SECURITY

Engineered for Efficiency & Safety

O(1) Small-Leg & Big-Leg Engine

Calculates small leg volume instantly (totalTeamVolumeUsd - maxLegVolumeUsd) without looping through hundreds of downlines, ensuring exceptionally low gas fees.

Real-Time 300% Cap Reservation

Incoming network bonuses instantly update recipient quotas in real-time. Reaching 300% deactivates staking automatically and passes excess bonuses upward.

Anti-Circular Referral Protection

The bindSponsor() function automatically validates upline trees to prevent circular loops (A → B → A) that could disrupt network incentives.

OpenZeppelin Security Standard

Secured using industry-standard battle-tested libraries including ReentrancyGuard, Ownable2Step, Pausable, and SafeERC20.
